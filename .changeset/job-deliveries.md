Job deliveries: `alma-job-delivery/1` envelopes (`signJobDelivery`, `verifyJobDelivery`) record that a hired agent returned a result, by its digest; `envelopeLeafHash` accepts them. `jsonDigest` and `canonicalJsonValue` give the RFC 8785 canonical form and SHA-256 of any JSON value.
